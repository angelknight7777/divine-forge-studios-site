Support

Need help with a Divine Forge Studios game? Send us an email and include the game name, your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of the issue.

support@divineforgestudios.com

What to include

  • Game name
  • Device model
  • iOS or Android version
  • What happened and what you expected to happen

Security

Please never send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive credentials in a support request.
