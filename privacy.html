Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Divine Forge Studios s.r.o. respects your privacy. This policy explains the basic privacy practices for this website and will be updated as our mobile games are released.

Website data

We do not intentionally collect personal information through this website unless you choose to contact us. If you email us, we receive the information you include in your message and use it to respond and provide support.

Mobile games

Our mobile games may use third-party services such as app stores, advertising, analytics, or crash reporting. The exact services and data practices for each released game will be disclosed in the applicable app-store privacy information and reflected here before or at launch.

Advertising and consent

If a game uses advertising, consent choices required by applicable law will be presented where necessary. Depending on the user's choices and region, advertising providers may process device or usage information to provide, measure, or limit advertising.

Children

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website. Age-related requirements for individual games will be handled according to their features, store rating, and applicable law.
